**Full description:**
The bullies are everywhere - and you are the geeky kid trying to get out of school alive.

Creep through spooky 3D hallways and classrooms, watch the corner map to see where the bullies are, and duck into lockers or under desks to stay out of sight. Every level has a different winding route to the exit, and more bullies on patrol.

* 24 levels with a new bully to beat in each round
* Boss fights in the playground - punch, kick, jump and dodge
* Four different bullies, each tougher than the last
* Final escape: 24 bullies, one huge maze, one chance
* Intense, spooky soundtrack
* Easy touch controls: joystick to move, swipe to look, one tap to hide

Can you escape Bully Escape?
